Dialogue Formatting Guide

Clear, correct dialogue in a few easy rules.

Dialogue Formatting Guide

  1. Quotation marks. Wrap spoken words in quotes: "Come with me," she said.
  2. New speaker, new paragraph. Start a fresh line each time someone else talks.
  3. Punctuation inside. Commas and periods sit inside the closing quote.
  4. Tags and beats. Use said for most lines, or an action beat instead of a tag.
  5. Interruptions. Use an em dash when speech is cut off: "But I thought—"
  6. Read it aloud. Real people use short, uneven sentences.
