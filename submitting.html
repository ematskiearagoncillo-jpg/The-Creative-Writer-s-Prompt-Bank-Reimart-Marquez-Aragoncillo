A beginner's path to sending work out.

Submitting to Journals

  1. 1. Polish. Finish and edit your piece before sending.
  2. 2. Research. Read the journal to see if your work fits its style.
  3. 3. Read guidelines. Check word count, format and reading periods.
  4. 4. Write a cover letter. Keep it brief, with a short bio and the title.
  5. 5. Submit and track. Record where and when you sent it.
  6. 6. Be patient. Expect rejection often; revise and send again.
