Plot Outline Structure

A simple five-part path from first page to last.

Plot Outline Structure

  1. 1. Exposition. Introduce the character, world and normal life.
  2. 2. Inciting Incident. Something disrupts the normal and sets the story moving.
  3. 3. Rising Action. Obstacles grow and stakes get higher.
  4. 4. Climax. The biggest confrontation or decision.
  5. 5. Resolution. Show the aftermath and what has changed.
